Scan to PDF: Capture or Upload Photos and Build One Document

You do not always have a scanner. A phone camera is close enough for many everyday needs: an ID card, a school certificate, a signed form or a bill. The challenge is turning a pile of photos into one file that an office will accept.

The Scan to PDF tool on AllToolsPro lets you capture pages with your camera, or pick photos you already have, then combines them into a multi-page PDF. It is a straightforward tool without scanning filters, so a few good habits make a big difference.

What the tool offers

There are two ways to add pages: Use Camera, which opens a live camera view (the rear camera is requested on phones), and Upload Photos, which lets you choose several images from your device. Every page appears as a thumbnail with a remove button, and a Create Scanned PDF button builds the file.

Each image is placed on its own A4 portrait page and stretched to fill it.

How to scan step by step

  1. Place the document on a flat, plain surface in good light.
  2. Press Use Camera and allow camera access when your browser asks. Alternatively choose Upload Photos to add pictures you already took.
  3. Press the round capture button for each page. Every capture is added to the thumbnails below.
  4. Remove any bad shot with the small button on its thumbnail, then capture it again.
  5. When all pages are in, press Create Scanned PDF. The file is saved as Scanned_Document_AllToolsPro.pdf.

Pages appear in the order you add them, and there is no drag-to-reorder option, so capture them in sequence.

Practical uses

  • Job and exam applications: Scan marksheets, certificates and ID proof for upload portals.
  • Bank and insurance forms: Send filled and signed pages as a single file.
  • Students: Turn handwritten notes and assignments into a shareable PDF.
  • Small business: Keep a digital copy of supplier bills and delivery challans.
  • Home records: Store rent agreements, warranty cards and medical prescriptions.

Tips for a clearer scan

  • Fill the frame with the page and hold the phone parallel to the paper. Because pages are stretched to A4, a sheet photographed at an angle or in landscape will look distorted.
  • Use daylight or an even lamp and avoid shadows from your hand.
  • Put the paper on a dark, plain surface so edges are easy to see.
  • For the sharpest result, take photos with your phone's own camera app first and use Upload Photos, since the live view may give a lower resolution.
  • Check each thumbnail before creating the PDF.

Troubleshooting

Camera access denied or not available

The tool shows this message when permission is blocked or no camera is found. Allow camera access in your browser's site settings and try again, or use Upload Photos.

The page looks stretched

The tool fits each image to the full page, so the photo should be roughly portrait and A4-shaped. Crop in your gallery first.

Photos fail to appear

Use ordinary JPG photos. Other formats may not work reliably.

Privacy and security

Camera frames and uploaded photos stay in your browser, and the PDF is created on your device. The camera works only after you give permission. Close the tab when you finish and remove saved photos of IDs you no longer need.

Limits you should know

This tool does not detect page edges, crop, straighten, brighten, convert to black and white or recognise text. It is a quick camera-to-PDF assembler, not a full scanner app. For crisp, searchable results you may prefer a dedicated scanning app with OCR, and then use this page for simple jobs.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Scan to PDF tool on AllToolsPro free to use?

Yes. Scan to PDF is completely free on AllToolsPro. There is no registration, no signup and no watermark, and it works on mobile phones and computers.

Can I use my phone camera directly?

Yes. Press Use Camera, allow access, and capture each page. The rear camera is requested on phones.

Can I upload photos instead of using the camera?

Yes. Upload Photos accepts multiple images from your device.

Does it automatically crop or enhance scans?

No. Images are placed on A4 pages as they are, stretched to fill the page, with no cropping, filters or text recognition.

Can I reorder pages?

There is no reorder option. You can remove a page and add it again in the right sequence.

Are my scans uploaded to a server?

The page works in your browser, and the PDF is generated on your device.
