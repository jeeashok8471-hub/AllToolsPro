Text to PDF: Make a Styled PDF from Plain Text in Seconds

By AllToolsPro · Updated 12 October 2026 · 4 min read

Not every document needs a full word processor. A short notice, a poem, a set of rules, a handwritten-style note or a quick list is often just text. You still might need it as a PDF so it can be shared or printed without fuss.

The Text to PDF tool on AllToolsPro is built for that. You type, see a live view of the page, tweak a few style options and download. It is deliberately basic, and this guide explains what you can control.

What you can customise

The control panel is titled Design Your PDF and offers four settings:

  • Page Style: Standard White, Diary (Lined), Maths (Grid) or Custom Color.
  • Background Color: A colour picker. Choosing a colour switches the page style to Custom Color.
  • Font Size: Small, Medium or Large.
  • Text Color: Another colour picker for the text.

The text box accepts Hindi and English, and the Live View panel shows the page as you type.

Step-by-step

  1. Open the tool and click the text box that says Type your text here (Hindi/English). Type or paste your content.
  2. Choose a Page Style. Lined pages suit notes and letters, grid pages suit sums and rough work.
  3. Set Font Size and Text Color, and pick a Background Color if you want a tinted page.
  4. Look at Live View to confirm it looks right.
  5. Press Download PDF to save AllToolsPro_Personal_Doc.pdf.

Who finds it useful

  • Students: Turn typed notes or an essay into a lined or grid page for submission or printing.
  • Teachers: Make a quick class notice, worksheet instruction or rule list.
  • Shopkeepers and offices: Print a simple price list, a closure notice or a message in Hindi.
  • Writers: Save a short poem, story or quote as a shareable page.
  • Families: Create a handwritten-looking recipe or a letter on diary-style paper.

Tips for good results

  • Pick a high-contrast combination. Dark text on a light background is easiest to read and print.
  • Use Large font for short notices and Small for longer notes, so they fit on fewer pages.
  • Leave blank lines between paragraphs, since there is no heading or bold formatting.
  • Check the last page of long text for awkward breaks.
  • Keep a copy of your text somewhere, since the page does not save your work.

Troubleshooting

The PDF looks different from the live view

The PDF is generated from the live view, so they should match closely. If a line is cut at the bottom of a page, shorten the text or choose a smaller font.

The page is dark and hard to read

Check both colour pickers. A dark background with dark text is a common slip.

Hindi text looks odd

Hindi uses fonts available in your own browser and device, so appearance can vary between devices.

Privacy and security

Your text stays in your browser, and the PDF is created on your device. The page does not ask you to sign in. Even so, do not leave private passages on a shared computer after downloading.

Limits

This is a plain-text tool. You cannot add images, tables, bold headings, page numbers or custom fonts. The output is an A4 portrait page generated from an image of the preview, so text in the PDF may not be selectable. If you need proper formatting, use a word processor and export to PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Does Text to PDF support Hindi?

Yes. The text box is meant for Hindi and English. How Hindi looks depends on the fonts available in your browser.

What page styles are available?

Standard White, Diary (Lined), Maths (Grid) and Custom Color, plus a background colour picker.

Can I change the font?

No. You can change font size between Small, Medium and Large, and the text colour, but not the typeface.

What size is the PDF page?

A4 portrait.

Is my text uploaded?

The tool runs in your browser, and the page does not require a sign-in.
